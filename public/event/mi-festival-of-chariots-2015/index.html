MI Festival of Chariots 2015

admin June 13, 2015June 28, 2015
Event Start Date:
July 19, 2015		Event End Date:
July 19, 2015		Event Venue:
Novi Civic Center

This year the popular Festival of Chariots known as Rathyatra will take place on Sunday, July 19, 2015. Festivities start at 11 am and will go on up to 6pm. The parade starts at 11 am and will continue for ab0ut an hour. Don’t miss this opportunity to witness this spectacular event filled with bliss.
